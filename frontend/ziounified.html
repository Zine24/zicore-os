ZIO Unified

Unified 3D printing control system. ESP32-S3 camera, Marlin firmware, desktop visualizer, Android app, and CNC control — all integrated into a single platform.
ZIO Unified is the original 3D printing module now integrated into ZICORE Aerospace. It combines ESP32-S3 camera streaming, Marlin firmware control, CNC node management, and a desktop visualizer into one cohesive system.

Repository: github.com/Zine24/ZioUnified
